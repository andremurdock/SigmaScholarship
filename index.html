Our mission

Scholarships make a college degree possible for students who have already done the hard part.

The 50 for 50 Sigma Scholarship Foundation, Inc. is a Georgia nonprofit corporation dedicated to empowering students in the Metro Atlanta area who are seeking a college education.

We raise money in the community, we help students find and pursue the awards they qualify for, and at the close of each year we put scholarship dollars directly toward their education.

What we do

Three things, done well

Every activity of the Foundation falls into one of three pillars.

01

Community philanthropy

Our volunteers host fundraising activities and solicit donations from individuals, businesses, and community partners across Metro Atlanta. Every dollar raised is earmarked for students.

02

Scholarship awareness

Money goes unclaimed every year because students never hear about it. We educate students and families on the scholarship opportunities available to them and push them to apply.

03

Scholarship dissemination

At the close of each year we award scholarships to qualifying students, putting the funds we raised to work on tuition, books, fees, and the costs that quietly derail a degree.

For students

If you are headed to college, start here.

Our application is open to Metro Atlanta-area students pursuing a college education. You do not need a perfect transcript — you need a plan and the willingness to see it through.

  • Open to graduating high school seniors and current college students in the Metro Atlanta area
  • Funds are paid toward tuition, books, and educational expenses
  • Applications reviewed by the Foundation’s scholarship committee
  • Awards announced at the close of the year
