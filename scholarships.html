Our awards

What the scholarship covers

Funds are applied toward the real costs of staying enrolled.

Tuition & fees

Paid toward the student’s balance at their college or university so the award goes where it counts.

Books & materials

Textbooks, lab fees, software, and the course materials that quietly add up each semester.

Gap costs

The small balances — a registration hold, a housing deposit — that keep otherwise-ready students from re-enrolling.

Award amounts and permitted uses are set annually by the scholarship committee.

Eligibility

Who we award

Our scholarships support young men graduating high school across the Metro Atlanta area who are headed to college. The committee weighs academic standing, community involvement, and a clear plan for the degree.

  • Young man graduating high school in the Metro Atlanta area
  • Accepted to or planning to enroll at an accredited college, university, or technical school
  • Minimum GPA of {{MIN_GPA}}
  • Complete application submitted by the posted deadline

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I have to be connected to Phi Beta Sigma to apply?

No. Scholarships are open to eligible Metro Atlanta students regardless of any fraternity or sorority affiliation. The Xi Chi Sigma Chapter is our presenting partner, not an eligibility requirement.

Who reviews applications?

The Foundation’s volunteer scholarship committee reviews every complete application. Board members recuse themselves from any application where a conflict exists.
