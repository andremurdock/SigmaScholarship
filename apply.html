The process

How it works

Check that you qualify

Review the eligibility requirements below. If you are a Metro Atlanta student headed to or already in college, you are most likely in scope.

Gather your documents

Transcript, proof of acceptance or enrollment, and one letter of recommendation. Have them saved as PDFs before you start.

Write your statement

A short personal statement telling us what you are studying, why, and what finishing the degree makes possible. {{ESSAY_PROMPT}}

Submit by the deadline

Applications close {{APP_DEADLINE}}. Late or incomplete applications are not reviewed — give yourself a week of margin.

Hear back

The scholarship committee reviews every complete application. Recipients are notified and awards announced {{AWARD_DATE}}.

Eligibility

Before you start

  • Resident of the Metro Atlanta area
  • Graduating high school senior or currently enrolled college student
  • Accepted to or enrolled at an accredited college, university, or technical school
  • Minimum GPA of {{MIN_GPA}}

Required documents

What to attach

  • Official or unofficial transcript
  • Acceptance letter or proof of current enrollment
  • One letter of recommendation
  • Personal statement ({{ESSAY_LENGTH}})
